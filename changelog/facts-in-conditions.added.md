A task's `when`, `when_or` and `when_cel` can read the stored facts of the device it runs on with `pleiades run`, by a key written in the runbook (`facts["os.family"] == "debian"`), with what the run's own earlier tasks gathered on top. A key nothing has gathered on the device fails the task rather than reading as false, a fact never reaches a parameter or any output, and on the Walk tier a runbook reading one is refused before any device is contacted.
