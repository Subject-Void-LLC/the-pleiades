`pleiades onboard --tls-first-use` (and the Controller's onboard route with `tls_first_use=true`, which also needs `inventory:assert`) records the certificate a self-signed device presents as its pin, only where nothing in its record trusts it and no earlier onboarding verified another certificate, listed for review and warned about on every run until `pleiades confirm-tls-pin` (`--json` for the same result as one document, or `POST /inventory/devices/{name}/tls-pin/confirm`, scopes `inventory:assert` and `inventory:read`) confirms it into `tls_fingerprint`. Setting or removing `tls_fingerprint` or `tls_ca_pem` through `set-host` or a sync removes the first-use pin.
