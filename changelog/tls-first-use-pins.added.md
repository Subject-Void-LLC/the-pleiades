`pleiades onboard --tls-first-use` (and the Controller's onboard route with `tls_first_use=true`) records the certificate a self-signed device presents as its pin, only where nothing in its record trusts it, listed for review and warned about on every run until `pleiades confirm-tls-pin` (or `POST /inventory/devices/{name}/tls-pin/confirm`, scope `inventory:assert`) confirms it into `tls_fingerprint`.
