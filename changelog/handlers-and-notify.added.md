Added `handlers:` and `notify:`: a handler runs on a device only when a task that notifies it (by name or a `listen:` topic) changed something there, once however many tasks did, at the end of `pretasks`, `tasks` and `posttasks`, in the order the handlers are written. `meta: flush_handlers` is not supported yet. A handler with its own `target:`, one whose call acts on no device, and one with `lock_acquisition: all_at_plan_time` are refused, since none could ever run.
