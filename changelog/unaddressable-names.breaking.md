A device can no longer be created under the name `""`, `.` or `..`, and a new fact can no longer be named `.` or `..`, since no URL can carry them as a path segment and such a record could never be opened, changed or deleted by its address. `POST /inventory/devices`, the kubeconfig import, the web UI's device form, `pleiades add-host` and `pleiades import-kubeconfig` refuse such a name and say why, and `pleiades inventory sync` reports such an upstream device as refused and adds the rest; records already stored are not changed.
