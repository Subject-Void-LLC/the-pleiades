A rollback now leaves a change a block's `always:` task made in place, and lists it once as a cleanup with the reason (`cleanups` in `pleiades rollback --json` and in the Controller's answer), where before it refused such a change with no undo until it was named with `--leave`, and replayed one with a recorded exact undo, which put back what the cleanup removed. Give the `always:` task a `rollback:` list to have it undone; a later run whose only change left is such a cleanup is refused as `left_in_place` and stepped over with `--despite-run`.
