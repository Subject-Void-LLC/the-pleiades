`pleiades validate` now checks the entries of `net.batfish.check`'s `devices`, `assertions`, `scenarios` and `accept_unrecognized`: a key no entry takes (such as the half of an unquoted reason a YAML flow mapping split at a comma), a missing required key, or a value of the wrong type is refused before the run, naming the entry. A method can declare its structured parameters' entries, and the module reference lists them.
