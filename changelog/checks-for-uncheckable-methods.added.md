Nine methods that could not be checked now can. `virt.clab.lab.restart`, `lab.redeploy`, `lab.save`, `node.restart`, `node.ssh_access` and `node.disable_tx_offload` read the lab or node they would act on and predict the change; `veth.create`, `vxlan.create` and `node.disable_tx_offload` also confirm the login is a superuser, which the server requires. `exec.winrm.shell` takes `creates` and `removes`, as Ansible's `win_shell` does, so a guarded call is checked by reading its guard, and a guard path the device cannot read fails the task rather than counting as absent.
