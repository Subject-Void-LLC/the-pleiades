A package, unit or service name that begins with `-`, or holds `*`, `?`, `[` or `]`, is refused for every `pkg.*`, `pkg.apt.*`, `pkg.dnf.*`, `svc.*`, `svc.systemd.*` and `svc.windows.*` method: by `pleiades validate` when it is written in the task, and before anything is sent when it is rendered from a variable or a registered result. Before, apt-get, dnf and systemctl could read such a name as an option or as a pattern matching many packages or units, and `pkg.apt.install` with a name ending in `-` removed the package; every name now also follows a `--` on the command line, and a `dpkg-query` or `rpm` that fails to answer fails the task instead of reading as "not installed".
