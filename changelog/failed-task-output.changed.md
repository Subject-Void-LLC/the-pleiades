A failed task keeps the stats its method recorded before it failed: a failed `exec.command`, `exec.shell` or `exec.winrm.shell` reports its exit status, standard output and standard error in `pleiades run` and `adhoc`'s `--json` and under `--verbose`, as Ansible does. The Runner's per-task child and the external Collection protocol carry them too, though a Controller job still shows a failed task by its error and warnings. Its recorded undo is left out, nothing is registered, and a task held for review shows nothing.
