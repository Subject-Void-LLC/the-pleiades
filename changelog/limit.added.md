Added `limit` on the Controller and `--limit` on `pleiades run` and `adhoc`: Ansible's `--limit` grammar (names, tags, groups including their child groups, globs, `!` to exclude, `&` to intersect), read by one matcher on both tiers, before any device is admitted. A limit matching none of the devices, or made only of separators (`,`), fails rather than running nothing or everything, a rollback ignores it, and for a playbook job the Controller applies it instead of passing `--limit` to ansible-playbook.
