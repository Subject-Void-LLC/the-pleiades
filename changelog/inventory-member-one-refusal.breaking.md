`POST /inventories` and `PATCH /inventories/{id}` answer a group or device that belongs to another organization (or a group holding one) with the same 400 and the same text as one that does not exist: "one or more of the groups or devices do not exist or cannot be added to this inventory". It used to be a 403 naming another organization. This removes the difference between the two answers; it does not make ids private, since until per-organization authorization exists a caller can still name another organization on create, and the web UI's membership chooser lists every tenant's devices and groups. A PATCH now checks only the members it adds: a member the inventory already holds is not checked again, so one that has since moved to another organization no longer blocks a rename (and no job reaches it).
