A wildcard in a package, unit or service name (`pkg.dnf.install` with `name: "kernel-devel-*"`) is now refused rather than passed to the package or service manager, and so is a `pkg.apt.*` name ending in `-`; name each package or unit on its own task, or loop over the names. The `owner` and `group` of `file.copy`, `file.template`, `file.directory`, `file.permissions` and `file.touch` are held to the same rule; useradd and groupadd refuse such names anyway, so only an account or group made some other way is affected.
