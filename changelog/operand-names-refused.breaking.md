A wildcard in a package, unit or service name (`pkg.dnf.install` with `name: "kernel-devel-*"`) is now refused rather than passed to the package or service manager, and so is a `pkg.apt.*` name ending in `-`; name each package or unit on its own task, or loop over the names. The `owner` and `group` of `file.copy`, `file.template`, `file.directory`, `file.permissions` and `file.touch`, and the `name` and primary `group` of the `identity.user.*` and `identity.group.*` methods, are held to the same rule, so an account or group whose name holds `*`, `?`, `[` or `]`, which useradd and groupadd make on Debian 12 and Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, can no longer be named there.
