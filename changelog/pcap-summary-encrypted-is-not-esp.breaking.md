`net.pcap.summary` counts SSH, TLS, HTTP/1.x and HTTP/2 packets under `ssh`, `tls`, `http`, `http2` and `grpc` instead of `tcp`, and SSH and TLS now add to `encrypted` and `key_exchange`, so a `when:` reading `tcp`, `encrypted` or `key_exchange` may change. `encrypted` is no longer the IPsec count: read `protocols.esp` (and `esp-udp`) to ask whether traffic crossed inside the tunnel.
