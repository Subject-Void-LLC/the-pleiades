`pleiades validate`, a Runner before it runs a dispatch, and a Controller's admission now refuse a `wait.routes` task on a device that is neither an FRR router (FRRCapable and CommandExecCapable, over SSH) nor a Cisco IOS one, where before it failed only once the task ran. A method can now declare the kinds of device it serves as alternatives, each with its own capabilities and transports, and `pleiades doc` and the module reference list them.
