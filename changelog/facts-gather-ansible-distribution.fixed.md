`facts.gather` now reports `ansible_distribution`, its version and its release as Ansible does, so a converted `when: ansible_distribution == "Debian"` holds on Debian: for example `Debian` and `12.12` (the point release) rather than `Debian GNU/Linux` and `12`, `Rocky` rather than `Rocky Linux`, `RedHat` rather than `Red Hat Enterprise Linux`, and `Alpine` rather than `Alpine Linux`. On Windows memory is rounded up and swap is `0`, as Ansible reports them, and macOS no longer reports a swap total, which Ansible leaves out. On the first gather after upgrading, a stored `ansible_distribution` (and Debian's version, Windows' swap and memory) records one change, and a Mac's swap fact reads as removed once. The distribution change applies to every build; the rest affect only builds after 0.3.0.
