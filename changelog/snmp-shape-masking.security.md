SNMP communities and SNMPv3 passphrases are masked by their shape in every output the platform masks, keeping the keyword so a line still reads `snmp-server community ******** RO`: IOS and NX-OS `snmp-server community`, `snmp-server host` and `snmp-server user` lines (NX-OS's `sha-256`, `aes-128` and localized keys included), net-snmp's `rocommunity`, `rwcommunity`, `com2sec`, `createUser`, `trapsink`, `trap2sink`, `informsink`, `trapcommunity` and `trapsess` lines, and snmptrapd's `authCommunity`. Network configuration a method reads masks the same lines, and no longer reads a host line's `vrf` as its community or an NX-OS algorithm as its passphrase, which left the secret after each in the clear; output in any other shape, such as `show snmp community`, is masked by value only, so a community shorter than 8 characters is shown there.
