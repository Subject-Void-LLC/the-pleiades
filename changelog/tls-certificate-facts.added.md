Onboarding a `generic_http`, `generic_restconf`, `generic_gnmi`, `generic_grpc`, `container_host`, `ai_provider` or `cloudflare_account` device over TLS records the certificate it presented (both fingerprints, subject, issuer and last day) as discovery facts, and `net.gnmi.facts`, `net.restconf.facts`, `net.gnmi.config` and `net.restconf.config` record it as stable `tls.certificate.*` facts; a `kubernetes_cluster` records none. Each takes the certificate from a handshake that completed, never one still under way. Those onboardings and those four methods, and nothing else, warn when the certificate is within 30 days of its last day, or past it under a pin, or when a pin of a list matched nothing.
