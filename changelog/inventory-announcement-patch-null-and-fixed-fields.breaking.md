`PATCH /inventories/{id}` and `PATCH /announcements/{id}` refuse a null on any field with a 400 that names it; leave a field out to keep it, or send an empty value to clear it. A null member list used to keep the stored members with a 200, and a null on any other field cleared it or answered 500. Both refuse a field named twice in any mix of upper and lower case, and an `organization` that differs from the stored one, with a 400 naming it, where a different organization used to be accepted and ignored with a 200; sending the stored value, 0 for a system-wide announcement, is still accepted. `POST /inventories` and `PATCH /inventories/{id}` answer 400 for a group or device that does not exist, and `POST /inventories` and `POST /announcements` answer 400 for an organization that does not exist, where the inventory routes answered 409 saying the name was already taken and the announcement route answered 500. A device or group named twice in an inventory's list is now kept once, where it answered 409.
