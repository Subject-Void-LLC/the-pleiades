`pleiades validate` and the Runner's preflight now refuse a task whose device lacks a capability its method requires, and a Controller skips such a device at launch, as it now also skips a device that cannot reach any of a method's transports. Before, only the legacy transport actions (`ssh_exec`, `winrm_exec` and four more) were checked this early, and every other method's requirements were found only once the run reached the task. A task with a `when:` of its own is left to the engine, which checks it on each device where the condition holds, since the run may skip it anywhere; `pleiades validate` used to refuse such a task when its device could not reach the method's transports, and no longer does. A launch whose every device is skipped this way completes with nothing dispatched and nothing failed, and each device's skip reason names the method that refused it.
