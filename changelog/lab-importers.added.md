Added `pleiades import-gns3` and `pleiades import-cml`, which make a GNS3 project's or a Cisco Modeling Labs lab's nodes devices from the server's own API. `import-gns3` speaks GNS3's 3.x and 2.2 APIs and makes each node with a telnet console a `console_device` at that console. `import-cml` makes IOS and IOS XE node definitions `cisco_router` or `cisco_switch` devices and the rest `generic_ssh`, at the address the controller observed or `--address` gives, never one guessed from a configuration, and verifies the controller by a pinned certificate fingerprint (`--tls-fingerprint`) or CA (`--tls-ca`). A password is read from standard input or the environment, never a flag, and is refused over plain HTTP to another machine unless `--allow-plaintext` is given.
