The fact families reserved since 0.3.0 are now named here, `ssh.` among them: `config.`, `file_attached.`, `gnmi.`, `netconf.`, `restconf.`, `snmp.`, `ssh.`, `tls.` and `yang.`, and `ansible_`. Reserving a family also reserves its namespace, so an external Collection can no longer use `config`, `file_attached`, `gnmi`, `netconf`, `restconf`, `snmp`, `ssh`, `tls` or `yang` for a method or a capability, nor declare a fact in any of those families. After the upgrade, an installed program that does is refused when Collections are loaded, and none of the programs in its directory load until it moves to a namespace of its own, as for the families the first-party reservation entry names (`os`, `host`, `hardware` and the rest). None of these was reserved in 0.3.0, and a family is added to the list only on a minor release.
