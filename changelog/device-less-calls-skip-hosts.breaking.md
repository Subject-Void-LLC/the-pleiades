A task whose method acts on no device for that call, such as `http.request` with a full URL, no longer runs once per device under a runbook's `hosts:` in `pleiades run`: it runs once, with no device and no credential, and its registered result is keyed by no device. `http.request` with a path on a device's API still runs against every device `hosts:` names. The Controller's Runner is unchanged for now.
