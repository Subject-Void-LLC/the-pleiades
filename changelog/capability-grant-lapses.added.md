A capability an external program defines can carry a `revision` its author bumps to withdraw every grant of it, a grant that grants nothing now says why (a changed definition, a repointed device, properties that no longer fit) wherever a run or `pleiades validate` needs it, and `validate` warns about a grant no loaded program defines. `pleiades revoke-capability --build <digest>` takes back every grant one program build probed, on every device, and keeps every other grant.
