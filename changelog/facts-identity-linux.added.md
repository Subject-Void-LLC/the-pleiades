`facts.gather` now records four identity facts on Linux: `os.machine_id`, `hardware.system_uuid`, `hardware.serial` and `hardware.virtualization` (`physical`, `vm:<hypervisor>`, `cloud:<provider>` or `container:<engine>`). Firmware placeholders such as "To be filled by O.E.M." are never stored, and the UUID and serial, which only root may read, are simply absent for a login without root. Azure and Hyper-V both read as `vm:microsoft`, since the system cannot tell them apart. FreeBSD, macOS and Windows do not report these yet.
