A service's state (running or stopped) is now its own fact, `service_state.<name>`, kept as its latest value only, since services start and stop on their own; `service.<name>` keeps `enabled`, `status` and `source`, and a `filter` naming `service.<name>` alone no longer returns the state. On macOS the jobs launchd makes for each connection and session are no longer listed as services, and on Windows the per-user service instances made for each signed-in user are no longer listed, while their templates are, marked `per_user`. Every stored service records one change on the first gather after upgrading.
