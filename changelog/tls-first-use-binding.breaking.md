A device whose discovery is bound to its TLS settings (`generic_http`, `generic_restconf`, `generic_gnmi`, `generic_grpc`, `container_host`, `kubernetes_cluster` and the other types that bind them) and was onboarded before this release is onboarded again once: its discovery is now bound to `tls_first_use_fingerprint` as well, and until then a run refuses it, naming `pleiades onboard`.
