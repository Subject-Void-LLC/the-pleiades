A grant of a capability an external program defines is now bound to the digest of its definition, so `POST /inventory/devices/{name}/capabilities` requires `definition` (the digest `pleiades doc <capability>` prints) and refuses a grant without it (`required-added openapi.json#/paths/~1inventory~1devices~1{name}~1capabilities/post/requestBody/content/application~1json/schema/properties/definition`). A grant written by 0.3.0, which carries no digest, grants nothing from 0.4.0 and says so until the device is onboarded or the assertion made again; and a program defining more than 64 capabilities, or a capability in a namespace another loaded program's methods also use, is refused when it loads.
