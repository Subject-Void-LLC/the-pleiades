A Controller now refuses to launch, check, relaunch or schedule a template whose runbook does not compile, answering with the builder's own message (422 for a launch, a check and a relaunch, 400 for a schedule's write) where it used to accept the launch and fail the job at fan-out with `runbook "x" could not be resolved`; creating a template over such a runbook is a 400 with the same message, where it was a 500. A schedule saved earlier whose runbook stops compiling records each occurrence as skipped with `launch_failed` and can still be renamed, paused and deleted, and a job whose runbook stops compiling between its launch and its fan-out carries the builder's message as its failure reason. `GET /runbooks/{id}` answers 400 with that message for such a runbook, where it said `invalid runbook id`, and a rollback of a job whose runbook the Controller cannot read answers 500 `internal error`, where its 422 carried the storage error and the server's runbook path. The message is masked by shape, so a credential-shaped value written into a runbook is not repeated in it.
