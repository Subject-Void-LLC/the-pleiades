A login a device refuses, an SSH host key `known_hosts` refuses or a WinRM certificate this side refuses no longer counts as the device being healthy: it never clears a run of failures to reach the device or closes an open circuit, so a device whose logins time out and are refused by turns (a failing TACACS+ or RADIUS server) now opens its circuit and stops being sent the credential, and when such a refusal answers the one call let through an open circuit, the next call is let through in its place.
