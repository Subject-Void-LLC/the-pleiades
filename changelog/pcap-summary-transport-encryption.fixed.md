`net.pcap.summary` reads each TCP connection from its own bytes, never its port, so SSH and TLS (NETCONF over SSH, RESTCONF over TLS) count as key exchange and encrypted rather than cleartext, unless what they frame as encrypted reads as text (a TLS NULL suite, the SSH none cipher), which is counted cleartext and flagged in `cleartext_in_encrypted`. HTTP/1.x, HTTP/2 and gRPC in the clear are named and reported as cleartext management, with any header carrying a credential named in `cleartext_credentials` by its name, never its value; credentials are searched in HTTP/1.x and HTTP/2 headers only (Telnet, FTP, SNMP communities and URL query strings are not searched), and `headers_unread` counts headers that could not be.
