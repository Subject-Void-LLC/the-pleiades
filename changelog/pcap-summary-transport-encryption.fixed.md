`net.pcap.summary` reads each TCP connection from its own bytes, never its port, so SSH and TLS (NETCONF over SSH, RESTCONF over TLS) count as key exchange and encrypted rather than cleartext. HTTP/2 and gRPC in the clear are named and reported as cleartext management in the new `flows`, `cleartext_management` and `warnings` results, with any header carrying a credential (`authorization`, `username`, `password` and the like) named in `cleartext_credentials` by its name, never its value.
