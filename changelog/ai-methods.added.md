New `ai.*` methods call a language model as a step of a run, on `ai_provider` devices (Ollama, Anthropic, Google, or any server speaking OpenAI's chat API). Each provider's key is its own credential and its residency (`local` or `hosted`) is part of its record. `ai.chat` asks one provider, optionally for a JSON-schema answer, and `ai.route` asks a use's providers in fallback order. A use tagged local is refused against a hosted provider at validate and again before contact. Every call ends in a usage record (tokens, time, retries, cost from the record's price table), never holding what was sent or answered, and can be appended to a project file. Parameters a kind does not accept are ignored, ones a provider rejects are dropped for the rest of the run, and only retryable failures are retried. Answers crossing a use's review thresholds (cost, tokens, a pattern, a JSON field, the stop reason, a fallback, an unsigned prompt) are held for a person: asked at the terminal, or kept in `ai/review` with the task failing as `held` until `pleiades ai review approve` or `deny`, after which running again reuses the decision with no second call. Prompts can be signed with Ed25519 (`pleiades ai prompt keygen` and `sign`, both refused without a terminal or under an agent) and verified against the project's trusted keys, with a monotonic version and expiry. `ai.provider.list` and `probe`, `ai.model.list`, `load`, `unload`, `pull` and `remove` (for an Ollama host whose record declares the single-resident guard), `ai.bench.run` and `ai.prompt.verify` complete the set. A check of any of them makes no model call. The run report gains a `held` task status, and the engine declares the pause and resume that will replace failing a held task.
