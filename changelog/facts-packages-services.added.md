`facts.gather` takes Ansible's `gather_subset` (`min`, `hardware`, `packages`, `services`, `all`, and `!x` to leave one out) and records each installed package as `package.<name>` and each service as `service.<name>`, on Linux, FreeBSD and macOS over SSH and on Windows over WinRM, where `windows_server` now gathers facts too. Ansible's `package_facts`, `service_facts` and `ansible.windows.setup` convert to it.
