Every WinRM call now goes through one circuit breaker per address and port, so after five failures in a row to reach a Windows host the tasks after them fail at once, saying `circuit open`, instead of each waiting out its timeout; `wait.connection`, `win.reboot` and `exec.winrm.shell`'s `expect_disconnect` reconnect are neither counted nor refused, and their success closes it. Over WinRM those waits send the credential only once the listener answers and stop at the first refusal, so a wrong password is sent once per wait rather than at every try.
