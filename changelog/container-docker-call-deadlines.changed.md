Every docker call `container.docker.run`, `stop` and `remove` make now has a deadline (30 seconds to read the engine or the container, 90 to stop, 60 to remove, 2 minutes to create or start), so a daemon that accepts a connection and never answers fails the task, saying which call and that a change it was making may or may not have happened, rather than holding the run and its device lock indefinitely. A pull, whose honest duration depends on the image and the link, has its own `pull_timeout` (seconds, default 900).
