A device that belongs to an organization is now reached only through that organization's inventories. A device unowned when an inventory took it in could later be put in another organization, and a group could later gain another organization's device; the inventory kept the member, and a job launched against that inventory, by hand or on a schedule, still dispatched to the other tenant's device with that device's own stored credential. Such a member now stays in the stored membership but no job reaches it, and resolving which inventories reach a device leaves the other organization's inventory out.
