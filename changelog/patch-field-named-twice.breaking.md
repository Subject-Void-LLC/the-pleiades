Every `PATCH` route that takes a partial update (templates, organizations, teams, contacts, role bindings, projects, credential types and credentials) refuses a body that names one field twice, in any mix of upper and lower case, with a 400 naming the field, where it used to accept it: a later key won for most fields, and for a credential's `external` and a template's `survey` both values were applied, so a body naming `external` twice stored both sets of references.
