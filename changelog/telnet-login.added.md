Added a Telnet back end that logs in: a device's stored credential answers its `Username:` or `login:` and `Password:` prompts, a refused or missing login is reported by name, and a device that asks nothing needs no credential. `net.ios.*` and `net.cli.*` run over Telnet as well as SSH, on a device whose record says `transport: telnet` (`cisco_router`, `cisco_switch`) and a task that sets `insecure_telnet: true`, since Telnet carries the login in cleartext. `import-cml --via telnet` imports a lab whose nodes run no SSH.
