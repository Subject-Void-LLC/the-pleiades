`facts.gather` no longer lists systemd units that last only as long as a login, a connection or a process, such as a socket-activated `sshd@<n>-<address>.service` for each SSH connection, `user@<uid>.service` and `user-runtime-dir@<uid>.service`, and transient units started for D-Bus, so a host's services no longer change between gathers when nothing changed. A lingering user's `user@` unit is left out too, and on systemd before 230, which does not mark transient units, those are still listed.
