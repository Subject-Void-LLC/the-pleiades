A `windows_server` can declare that it runs VirtualBox: `pleiades set-host <host> --set virtualbox=true`, with `vboxmanage_path` and `vm_folder` optional. The host keeps its credential and pinned authority, and becomes the target for VirtualBox methods. The Windows lab setup script's `-AllowVirtualBox` and `-VirtualBoxAutostart` grant a least-privilege account exactly what VirtualBox needs from a WinRM logon, each found by a failure on a real host.
