`validate` now refuses `lock_acquisition: all_at_plan_time` on a `rescue:` task, and `run` refuses it by the same validation before its first task (in `--mode check` too), so a runbook they passed may be refused; the run used to refuse it only on reaching the rescue, after a task had failed, and a rescue in a block that runs as one unit (one of its tasks runs somewhere other than the runbook's `hosts:`) may still take the lock. On the Walk tier the Controller runs no validation rule, so a launch or schedule of such a runbook is still accepted, and each Runner refuses its dispatch before any task runs.
