Added `--vault <dir>` and `--inventory <file>` to every command that reads the credential vault or the inventory, each overriding `--dir` for its own part only: the vault (`master.key`, `credentials.yaml` with named secrets, `seal.key`, and the facts digest key derived from the master key) is read from the `--vault` directory's `.pleiades/`, the inventory from the named file, and the journal, fact store and `run.lock` stay under `--dir`. A `--vault` with no master key is refused rather than given a new one.
