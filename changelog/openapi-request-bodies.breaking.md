The published OpenAPI document now describes the JSON body that fourteen routes take: creating and editing organizations, teams, contacts, users, role bindings, inventories and announcements. Those bodies were left out of the document, so a client generated from it did not know what to send. Each route always required its body, and none of them answers differently now: only the document changed, and a client generated from the new one sends a body it had to send anyway. The inventory and announcement PATCH bodies are partial updates with no required field, so they add no requirement. The document's change is named here as the compatibility check reports it: `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1announcements/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1bindings/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1contacts/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1inventories/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1organizations/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1teams/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1users/post/requestBody`, `request-body-added-required openapi.json#/paths/~1users~1{id}/patch/requestBody`.
