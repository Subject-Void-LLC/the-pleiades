The Pleiades speaks SNMP: a `generic_snmp` device type, onboarded from its agent's own answers, and the methods `net.snmp.facts`, `net.snmp.get`, `net.snmp.set` (read before and after, with an exact undo `pleiades rollback` replays) and `wait.snmp`. SNMPv3 is the default, with every USM authentication protocol and every privacy protocol but 3DES proven against net-snmp; SNMPv1 and SNMPv2c need `snmp_allow_cleartext_community: true` and MD5 or DES need `snmp_allow_legacy_algorithms: true` on the device, each warned about on every onboarding and run.
